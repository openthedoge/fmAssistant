## English Description

### What it is

A small tool I built for my own HITMAN 3 Freelancer runs. It has three features:

1. **Leader Lookup**: Pick 3 appearance traits plus 1 hair colour and instantly see the 2 or 4 possible leader candidates. Tap a portrait to grey it out once you have ruled that person out.
2. **Item Locations**: Find where to get 163 items across 19 maps. Searchable with space-separated keywords, e.g., `paris pistol`, `75R`, `gun`.
3. **Others**: Custom images (long-press a leader portrait to replace it with your own; it is auto-cropped to the original size, and you will be asked whether to revert when you disable the feature), and the About page contains AI templates.

Fully offline: all images and text are bundled inside the app, so it works without a connection. No analytics and no ads.

### About the Data (Important)

- All content (item locations, leader data, and images) was **collected by hand** by me over time from various websites and guide screenshots. **It is not guaranteed to be up to date.**
- Data last organized: **July 2026**.
- After a game update, some locations may have changed and I may not have caught up with the changes. **Inaccurate data is not a problem with this app** — always trust what you see in-game.
- Leader data and images come from [trashcan.fr/freelancer](http://trashcan.fr/freelancer/).
