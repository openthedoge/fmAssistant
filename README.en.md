## English

### What it is

A small tool I built for my own HITMAN 3 Freelancer runs. It has three parts:

1. **Leader Lookup** — pick 3 appearance traits plus 1 hair colour and instantly see the 2 or 4 possible leader candidates (72 leaders, 60 query combinations). Tap a portrait to grey it out once you have ruled that person out.
2. **Item Locations** — where to find 163 items across 19 maps. Searchable, with space-separated keywords, e.g. `paris pistol`, `75R`, `gun`.
3. **Others** — dark/light theme, custom images (long-press a leader portrait to replace it with your own; it is auto-cropped to the original size, and you are asked whether to revert when you disable the feature), and About.

Fully offline: all images and text are bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection. No analytics and no ads.

### About the data (important)

- All content — item locations, leader data and images — was **collected by hand** by me over time from various websites and guide screenshots. **It is not guaranteed to be up to date.**
- Data last organised: **July 2026**.
- After a game update, some locations may have changed and I may not have caught up. **Inaccurate data is not a problem with this app** — always trust what you see in-game.
- Leader data and images come from [trashcan.fr/freelancer](http://trashcan.fr/freelancer/).

### Maintenance

**Updates are not guaranteed.** I may reorganise the guides during holidays when I have time. Stars don't help me, so there's no need to bother.

If you have newer or more accurate guide data, feel free to tell me — or just modify it yourself.
